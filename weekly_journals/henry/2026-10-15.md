## What I Learned

- Character-count retention and exact message recovery measure different
  properties. A platform can preserve the total count while changing the
  sequence, so both results must be recorded.
- Retention above 100 percent is possible when a platform inserts or duplicates
  codec characters and should be investigated instead of capped.
- Hashing the corpus prevents accidental retyping or Unicode normalization from
  changing the production test inputs.
