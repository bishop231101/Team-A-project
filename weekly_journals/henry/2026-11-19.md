## Limitations confirmed

Hamming SECDED corrects a single substituted bit and detects two substituted
bits within one codeword. It cannot restore deleted or stripped symbols,
missing separators, reordered codewords, or a completely removed payload.
Zero-width encoding is also not encryption, and CRC-32 is not cryptographic
authentication.
