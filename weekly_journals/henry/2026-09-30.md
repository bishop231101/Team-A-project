## What I Learned

- A five-copy repetition group can correct at most two damaged symbols, which
  creates a controlled recovery boundary of 40% per group.
- A 100% character-survival rate does not guarantee successful decoding because
  altered characters remain present but may carry the wrong bit value.
- Safe failure is important: the checksum and structural checks prevent the
  decoder from returning damaged data as a valid secret.

## Problems Encountered

- Overall percentages can hide concentrated damage. A message may fail below
  40% if three changes occur in one group, while evenly distributed damage can
  remain recoverable through 40%.
- Removal, alteration, and mixed corruption require different survival-rate
  interpretations because alteration does not decrease the character count.
