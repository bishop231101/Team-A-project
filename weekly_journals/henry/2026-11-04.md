## Challenges and Limitations

Hamming corrects bit substitutions when symbol positions remain available. It
cannot recover deleted zero-width characters or a fully stripped payload. This
means it cannot recover the official Gmail results where U+200B was removed.
Those conditions are rejected safely instead of returning incorrect plaintext.
