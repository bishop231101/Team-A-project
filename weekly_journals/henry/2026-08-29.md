## What I Learned

- Zero-width characters can hide information because they are not normally visible to the reader.
- Platforms may preserve, remove, reorder, or alter invisible characters during copy and paste.
- A baseline round-trip test is needed before platform robustness can be measured.
- Repetition and Reed-Solomon or Hamming error correction can help recover a message when some characters are lost.
