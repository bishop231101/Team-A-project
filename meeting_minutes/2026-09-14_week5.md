### Week 5 Results

- Reviewed and confirmed completion of the Week 5 platform testing.
- Completed 27 platform tests across Microsoft Outlook, Telegram Web, and Notepad++.
- All three platforms preserved the tested zero-width characters, resulting in successful decoding of all 27 test messages.
- Reviewed the four recovery testing scenarios involving removal and alteration of zero-width characters.
- All four recovery tests resulted in decode failure, establishing a baseline for improving recovery in future testing.
- Confirmed that the automated test suite passed 19 tests and 108 subtests after resolving the Python package naming conflict.
- Reviewed Week 5 screenshots, documentation, journals, and supporting testing evidence.
- Confirmed completion of the Week 5 team deliverables.

### Problems Encountered

- A Python package naming conflict caused import issues during testing. The package was renamed from `code` to `src`, and the test imports were updated.
- Recovery testing showed that the current codec cannot successfully recover hidden messages when zero-width characters are removed or altered.
- The team identified that a high character survival rate does not necessarily guarantee successful decoding when character values are altered.

### Next Steps

- Begin Week 6 work using the Week 5 testing results as the baseline.
- Investigate redundancy methods to improve recovery of damaged zero-width payloads.
- Investigate error-correction techniques, including repetition and Reed-Solomon error correction.
- Compare future recovery results with the Week 5 baseline.
- Continue automated testing, documentation, individual weekly journals, meeting minutes, and GitHub integration.
